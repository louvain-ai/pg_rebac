# Code of conduct

This project is a shared workplace. Everyone who takes part in it (in issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions) is expected to make it one people want to come back to.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, never about the person.
- Be specific and constructive: say what is wrong and, where you can, what would be better.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, including ones that have been asked before.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not post anyone's personal information without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or repeatedly pushing a point after maintainers have closed it.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel ("Security" tab,
then "Report a vulnerability": it is private, and it is read for conduct reports too), or by contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may temporarily
or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful. They apply it fairly and consistently, and they are held
to it themselves.
